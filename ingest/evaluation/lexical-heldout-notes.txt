Inventory: 8 synthetic EPUB documents, 21 authored sections and 20 queries. There are 14 technical diagnostic queries (gate=false) and 6 existing lexical-contract controls (gate=true). Four queries expect no match: two technical boundary diagnostics and two ordinary/filter controls. Three queries use an explicit book filter. No scale background is authored here.

Label policy: diagnostic goldens express a proposed exact-literal policy for explicitly typed technical lexemes. Significant suffixes, flag prefixes and internal separators remain significant; a complete technical lexeme differs from a longer lexeme sharing its prefix. A whitespace spelling without those separators is a collision, not an equivalent technical spelling. Parentheses and assignment punctuation delimit the technical name in two source contexts. Multiple explicit technical terms must occur in the same section. These are proposed diagnostics, not assertions that an unstated feature policy is already a required contract. All technical labels remain gate=false, including successful matches under the old tokenizer.

The six gates use ordinary words, two editions with the same title, explicit document filters, and absent vocabulary. Their goldens require only the existing lexical matching and filter contracts. Each golden quote occurs exactly once within its authored section. Each section is short enough to remain one default chunk; no ranking tie requires finding more than two relevant sections for any query. All query and document identifiers are distinct within their respective namespaces.

The fixture challenges punctuation collisions, short and long switches, scoped names, underscores, dotted names, token boundaries, multi-term conjunction, ordinary-word regressions and filtered abstention. It does not specify case sensitivity, Unicode normalization, arbitrary punctuation syntax, semantic paraphrase matching, mathematical operators, URLs, paths, or source-code parsing. It makes no claim about every technical language or every token boundary policy.

All prose and labels are original model-authored synthetic data. This is a small independent blind candidate evaluation, not a human-authored dataset, a statistically representative benchmark, or proof of production retrieval quality. No private books, external text, model execution, application imports, tests, lint or performance measurements were used while authoring. Static text inspection can establish label consistency; compatibility and retrieval outcomes still require execution on Spark after the candidate and fixture are pinned.
